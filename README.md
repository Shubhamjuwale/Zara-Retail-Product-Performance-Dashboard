# Zara-Retail-Product-Performance-Dashboard
Developed an interactive retail analytics dashboard in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and helper columns to analyze product performance, pricing strategy, promotions, and seasonal trends for a Zara sales dataset.

Performed data cleaning and transformation by creating calculated/helper columns such as Total Sales, Price Segments (Budget, Mid-Range, Premium), Promotion Status, and Product Performance categories to improve business analysis and dashboard insights. 

Designed a professional monochrome dashboard layout with interactive filters and business-focused visualizations including category-wise sales analysis, promotion impact analysis, pricing segment contribution, and seasonal product performance tracking. 

Generated actionable retail insights by identifying top-performing product categories, analyzing promotion effectiveness, evaluating pricing strategy performance, and interpreting sales patterns to simulate real-world retail merchandising and business intelligence reporting workflows.
